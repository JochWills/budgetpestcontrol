Are quotes really free?

Yes. Tell us what you're dealing with and we'll quote it, with no obligation to go ahead.

Which areas do you cover?

Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth) and surrounds. If you're unsure whether we reach you, give us a call.

I'm selling my house. Do I need a wood borer inspection?

Often, yes. A wood borer inspection is commonly needed before a property transfer. Check with your conveyancer what's required, then get in touch to book it.

Do I need to leave the property during treatment?

It depends on the treatment. Most pest control doesn't need you to move out, while fumigation does. We'll explain exactly what to expect when we quote.

What should I tell you when I call?

What you're seeing, where it is (kitchen, roof, garden…), roughly how long it's been going on, and your suburb. Photos help too.
